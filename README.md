## Selected Research Projects

### Indoor PM₂.₅ Exposure During Smoke Events

Analysis of indoor and outdoor particulate-matter concentrations during smoke-impacted periods and evaluation of building-level exposure-reduction strategies.

### Indoor PM₂.₅ Filtration Modeling

Mass-balance modeling of indoor PM₂.₅ under different filtration and air-cleaning scenarios.

### Aerosol and Respiratory Deposition Analysis

Assessment of size-resolved particle behavior and respiratory deposited mass using aerosol and deposition modeling approaches.
